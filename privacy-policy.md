# Privacy Policy: Wishlist Exporter for Steam Next Fest

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Wishlist Exporter for Steam Next Fest ("the extension") exports your Steam wishlist to a
spreadsheet file on your computer. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What the extension reads

When you click **Export wishlist CSV**, the extension requests the following from Steam
(store.steampowered.com and api.steampowered.com), using your browser's existing Steam
sign-in:

- the list of games on your wishlist, and their wishlist rank and date added when your
  wishlist is public;
- public store information about those games (names, release dates, prices, reviews, tags,
  developers, publishers and demos);
- the current Steam Next Fest page and its list of demos.

If you choose to load Next Fest data files into the extension, it reads those files from
your computer.

## What it stores

- The optional Next Fest data you load (AI-disclosure, AI-category and studio information,
  keyed by game) is saved in your browser's local extension storage so you don't need to
  load it again. You can remove it at any time with the **Clear** link, or by uninstalling
  the extension.
- Your "Only games in Next Fest" setting is saved in local storage.
- The CSV file is created in your browser and saved to your computer like any download.

## What it does not do

- It does not send your wishlist or any other data to the developer or to any server other
  than Steam's own.
- It does not read your Steam password or cookies; requests to Steam are made by your
  browser with your existing session.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it does not sell or share data.
